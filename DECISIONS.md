# Decisions log

Every choice that the Master Handover Pack does not settle, or that departs from it, is recorded here.
Authority order when documents conflict: 03, 02, 04, 05, 06, 01, 07 (Doc 00 §2).

| # | Decision | Effect on the pack |
| --- | --- | --- |
